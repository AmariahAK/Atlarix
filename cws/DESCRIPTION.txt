Atlarix is an AI agent that works inside the browser you already use, with the sessions you are already signed into. Open the side panel, ask for something, and it reads the page, follows links, fills forms and pulls the answer together — without asking you to copy anything into a chat window.

YOU DECIDE WHERE IT WORKS
In the side panel, Atlarix asks the first time it touches a site, and every site it has worked on is listed and revocable from the panel or your account. Passwords, payments, downloads and anything destructive always stop and ask, whatever you have allowed. Atlarix also pairs with its own desktop app, and what you ask for there runs without a second prompt in the browser — you already asked for it in the app. You can narrow Atlarix to chosen sites from your browser's extensions page at any time.

IT FILLS FORMS, AND LEAVES SUBMITTING TO YOU
Unless you ask it to submit, it fills the form and stops.

WHAT YOU CAN DO WITH IT
• Read and summarise the page you are on, or a tab you have not opened yet
• Work across several tabs at once — compare, cross-reference, collect
• Let it click, type, scroll and take a screenshot to check what it sees
• Attach a document or a spreadsheet and ask about it. Atlarix reads the whole file, not the first page of it
• Ask for a relic: a self-contained chart, table or calculator it builds for you and you open in its own tab
• Ask for the answer as a file instead of a message, and it writes one you can download
• Pick a conversation back up on another device, because what was said follows your account

WHAT SYNCS, AND WHAT DOES NOT
Page text, and screenshots of the tabs Atlarix opened, go to the model to answer what you asked. They are never synced to your account: what syncs is what was said — your messages and its replies — so a conversation can be continued elsewhere. The files you attach stay on your device; only what the model reads from them is sent to it.

Every tab Atlarix opens is collected into a labelled group, so you can see at a glance what it has touched.
