Atlarix is an AI agent that works inside the browser you already use, with the sessions you are already signed into. Open the side panel, ask for something, and it reads the page, follows links, fills forms and pulls the answer together — without asking you to copy anything into a chat window.

YOU DECIDE WHERE IT WORKS
In the side panel, Atlarix asks the first time it touches a site, and every site it has worked on is listed and revocable from the panel or your account. Atlarix also pairs with its own desktop app, and what you ask for there runs without a second prompt in the browser — you already approved it in the app. You can narrow Atlarix to chosen sites, or switch it off for one, from your browser's extensions page at any time.

IT FILLS FORMS BUT NEVER SUBMITS THEM
Anything that sends, buys, posts or deletes stops and asks you first.

WHAT YOU CAN DO WITH IT
• Read and summarise the page you are on, or a tab you have not opened yet
• Work across several tabs at once — compare, cross-reference, collect
• Attach a document or a spreadsheet and ask about it. Atlarix reads the whole file, not the first page of it
• Ask for a relic: a self-contained chart, table or calculator it builds for you and you open in its own tab
• Ask for the answer as a file instead of a message, and it writes one you can download
• Paste a long block of text and it becomes an attachment instead of filling the box
• Pick a conversation back up on another device, because what was said follows your account

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE
The contents of pages Atlarix reads never leave your machine, and neither do the files you attach. What syncs to your account is what was said — your messages and its replies — so a conversation can be continued elsewhere. Page content is not part of that, is not stored on our servers, and is never used for training.

Every tab Atlarix opens is collected into a labelled group, so you can see at a glance what it has touched. Closing that group stops it.

WHAT IT COSTS
Atlarix requires an account and runs on Atlarix Core, our hosted model service, which is pay-as-you-go. The extension itself is free to install and there is no subscription required to use it, but running the agent spends credit from your balance, which you top up at atlarix.dev. You always see what a conversation is using in the panel, and Atlarix will not start a turn it cannot pay for.

Read how we handle data at https://atlarix.dev/privacy and https://atlarix.dev/data-use
